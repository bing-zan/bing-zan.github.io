Welcome欢迎

Bing Zan is a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Chemistry at King’s College London. She received her Ph.D. in Physics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2025 and was a visiting Ph.D. student at King’s College London from 2023 to 2025. Her research focuses on membrane-active peptides: from in silico mechanistic study, simulation-guided therapeutic peptide design and in vitro screening, to integrating peptides into microfluidic platforms for cancer cell detection. She aims to bridge fundamental biophysical mechanisms with practical peptide-based technologies for therapeutics and diagnostics.

昝冰现为伦敦国王学院化学系博士后研究员，主要研究膜活性多肽。 她于上海交通大学获得物理学博士学位，并在伦敦国王学院进行联合培养。她结合全原子分子动力学模拟与实验手段，系统研究多肽–脂质相互作用及多肽诱导的膜致孔机制。 其研究涵盖抗菌肽作用机理、多肽的模拟指导理性设计，以及多肽功能化微流控平台在无抗体癌细胞检测中的应用，并致力于连接基础生物物理机制与多肽治疗和诊断技术的应用。

Research 研究方向

• Mechanistic Understanding of Membrane-Active Peptides

Study of molecular mechanisms by which membrane-active peptides interact with and permeabilise lipid bilayers. Using all-atom molecular dynamics simulations, my work focuses on peptide–lipid interactions, peptide oligomerisation, and the formation and dynamics of membrane poration.

膜活性多肽与脂质膜相互作用及其致孔机制。通过全原子分子动力学模拟， 系统解析多肽-脂质相互作用、多肽聚集行为，以及膜中孔结构的形成机制和动力学。

• Simulation-Guided Design of Functional Peptides

Simulation-guided rational design of therapeutic peptides. This includes pH-responsive peptides with reversible membrane partitioning for targeting acidic tumour microenvironments, as well as lipid-selective peptides that preferentially bind cancer cell membranes.

在机制研究基础上，进一步发展基于模拟的多肽理性设计策略， 包括用于靶向肿瘤酸性微环境的 pH 响应型多肽，以及选择性识别癌细胞膜脂组成的脂质选择性多肽。

• Peptide-based microfluidic device for cancer cell detection

Peptide-functionalised microfluidic sensing platform for broad-spectrum cancer cell detection. The platform integrates optical and electrochemical readouts and enables detection from mixed cell populations, offering a scalable route toward non-invasive multi-cancer screening. A patent application has been filed in 2025.

我目前开发一种基于多肽功能化的微流控检测平台， 该体系可实现多种癌细胞的广谱检测， 并结合光学与电化学读出方式，为无创多癌筛查提供可扩展的解决方案。 相关技术已提交专利申请。
